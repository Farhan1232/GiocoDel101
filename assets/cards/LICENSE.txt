What was done to it
-------------------
The single source image is a 10 x 4 grid holding all forty cards. Each card
was extracted programmatically:

  * the printed artwork of each cell was isolated by connected-component
    analysis, so neighbouring cards' edge lines and scanner speckle are not
    carried over;
  * every card's artwork was composited, at one common scale, onto a white
    card of identical size (400 x 620 px, ratio 1.55, matching
    CardMetrics.aspect);
  * the result was resampled to 360 x 558 px and encoded as WebP, quality 92.

No artwork was redrawn, recoloured or restyled. Scale is uniform across the
deck, so a coin on the Sette is the same size as a coin on the Due.
